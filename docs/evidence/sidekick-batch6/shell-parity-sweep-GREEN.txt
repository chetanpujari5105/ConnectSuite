## Read this before quoting the focus-ring number

`835 of 835` is the **third** answer this sweep gave for the same fact, and the first two were both
the instrument:

| Reading | Stable? | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| `825 of 825`, then `824 of 825`, then `553 of 825` | no | the forced pseudo-state's style recalc lands on the renderer's own schedule, so `getComputedStyle` could resolve against the pre-recalc style |
| `557 of 829`, twice | **yes** | worse. Photos streams its thumbnails in and **replaces** its elements: the node count is unchanged, so a settle predicate on the count is satisfied, while every forced state now sits on a node no longer in the document. A stable, repeatable, completely wrong reading of 272 photo tiles as unringed |
| `835 of 835`, three consecutive runs | **yes** | force and read as one atomic pair, repeated until two consecutive pairs agree |

**A stable wrong answer is the dangerous one**, and it arrived looking exactly like a real defect —
272 controls in one pane with no focus ring is precisely the shape a pane that styles its own
controls would produce. What separated them was not picking the more trustworthy run: it was
re-applying the stimulus immediately before each sample, so the nodes carrying the state are the
nodes being read.

A fourth attempt is recorded in `probe.mjs` because it fails in a way nobody would predict: two
`requestAnimationFrame`s before reading. **rAF does not fire in an occluded window**, so the sweep
hung whenever Electron was behind another window — an instrument that works only while somebody is
looking at the app.
